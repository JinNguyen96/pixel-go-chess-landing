A quiet corner for Go

Pixel Go Chess is a quiet corner to learn and play the ancient board game of Go, also known as Baduk, Weiqi and Igo.

Pour a cup of tea and sit with an old cat sensei who walks you through the rules one small step at a time: liberties and captures, ko, eyes, life and death, and how to count the score.

What's inside

  • Tutorial

    32 short lessons in 5 chapters with the cat sensei, from your first stone to Japanese, Korean and Chinese rules.

  • Challenges

    22 life-and-death puzzles, each rated 1 to 3 stars, with hints when you get stuck.

  • Play the AI

    10 levels from beginner to about 1 dan on 9×9, 13×13 or 19×19, with hints, undo and game review.

  • Play people

    Two players on one device today. Online games are coming soon.

  • Three rule sets

    Play by Japanese, Korean or Chinese rules, each with its own komi and way of counting.

  • Daily rewards

    Daily check-in, daily quests and coins, with avatars and trophies to unlock.

  • Plays offline

    No account, no sign-in. Your games and progress stay on your device.

  • Made to feel cozy

    Hand-made pixel art, day and rainy-night themes, gentle animations and reduced-motion support.

  • 5 languages

    English, Tiếng Việt, 한국어, 日本語 and 简体中文.
